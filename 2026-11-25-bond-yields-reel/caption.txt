The bond market just hit its highest yields in 24 years. 📈

The US 30-year hit 5.59% on Sept 29, the highest since 2002. The 10-year topped 5.3% in October. This week it slid back below 5% as a tech selloff pushed money into Treasuries.

Why it matters: the 10-year yield sets the tone for mortgages, student loans and credit cards.
