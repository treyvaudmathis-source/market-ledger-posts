The Fed's favorite inflation gauge came in at 3.8% in October, nearly double its 2% target.
PCE tracks how much the prices households pay changed versus a year earlier. Core PCE, which excludes food and energy, was 3.3%.
Illustration: a basket costing $100 a year ago would cost about $103.80 at that average rate.
The Fed meets December 8-9; the gap to its 2% target is what it watches.
Sources: U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis (Personal Income and Outlays, Oct 2026, released Nov 25), Federal Reserve.
