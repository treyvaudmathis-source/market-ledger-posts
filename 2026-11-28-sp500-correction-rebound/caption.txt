The S&P 500 fell 11.6% from its Oct 6 record to its Nov 20 close, a market correction, then bounced 5.6% in a week to close at 7,297.49 on Nov 27.
A correction means a drop of 10% or more from a recent high. A fall of 20% or more is a bear market.
The catch: after an 11.6% fall you need a 13.1% gain to get back to the old high, because the gain starts from a smaller base.
Even after the bounce, the index is still 6.7% below its record. Analysts point to a rebound in battered AI stocks.
Sources: AP (Nov 27 close), S&P Dow Jones Indices closes; percentages computed.
